Trade marks forms and fees (Cap.559)

Trade mark applications can be filed electronically via our E-filing System 24 hours a day from Monday to Sunday. Read more

Trade mark applications can also be sent to "Trade Marks Registry, Intellectual Property Department, 24/F, Wu Chung House, 213 Queen's Road East, Wanchai, Hong Kong" by post or in person during the business hours 9:00 a.m. - 5:45 p.m. (Monday to Friday). Read more

Do not attempt to modify the forms, as doing so will result in an invalid form which cannot be accepted by the Registry.

Please pay the prescribed fee, if applicable, on submission of the form. We do NOT accept cash delivery (i.e. cash sent through mail). Read more

Important Reminder: Applicants and their employees or agents must not offer an advantage as defined in the Prevention of Bribery Ordinance (Cap. 201) to any government officer in connection with their applications or while having dealings of any kind with government departments.

Trade marks forms and fees (Cap.43)

If you wish to obtain a relevant form for Trade Marks Ordinance (Cap.43), please call our public enquiry hotline at 2961 6901 or write to us by fax (fax no. : 2893 3642) or at enquiry@ipd.gov.hk. We will revert to you as soon as possible.

You may also make the request via our public counter at 24/F, Wu Chung House, 213 Queen’s Road East, Wanchai, Hong Kong in the following office hours:

Monday to Friday: 9:00 a.m. to 5:45 p.m.

Please note that trade mark forms must be filed to the Trade Marks Registry by hand or by post.

Personal data collected by the Registrar of Trade Marks in the forms delivered to the Registrar will be used for the purposes set out in the Personal Information Collection Statement.
