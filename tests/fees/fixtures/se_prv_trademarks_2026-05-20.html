Fees - trademarks

Here you will find comprehensive information about fees for your trademark application.

Fees for government services are exempt from VAT. This applies to applications, registrations, copies and printouts of public documents.

Fees for Swedish trademark application

The fee must be paid regardless if your trademark application is approved or not, as it covers the handling costs. Application via e-service has a lower application fee.

If you choose to add goods or services that are not approved in the pre-approved list, the fee will instead be the same as when applying on paper or through another service.

Application for registration of a trademark for protection in one class, including a collective, guarantee or control trademark 2 700 SEK
For each additional class 1 000 SEK

Other service means applications that have been submitted in any other way than through PRV’s online application, for example applications submitted by e-mail.

Application for registration of a trademark for protection in one class, including a collective, guarantee or control trademark 3 900 SEK
For each additional class 1 000 SEK
Application of division of a trademark registration or application, for each divisional registration 1 800 SEK
Application for transfer of trademark ownership, for each application 1 000 SEK

Fees for changing, renewing and reinstatement a Swedish trademark

The fee must be paid regardless if your trademark application is approved or not, as it covers the handling costs. Application via e-service has a lower application fee.

Renewal application for protection in one class, including collective, guarantee or control mark 2 700 SEK
For each additional class 1 000 SEK
Increased fee for each class due to late renewal application 200 SEK
Application for registration of pledge agreement 1 800 SEK
Application for transfer of trademark ownership, for each registration 1 000 SEK
Application for revocation of trademark registration 900 SEK
Renewal application for protection in one class, including collective, guarantee or control mark 3 900 SEK
For each additional class 1 000 SEK
Increased fee for each class due to late renewal application 200 SEK
Application for transfer of trademark ownership, for each registration 1 000 SEK
Application for changes in a word mark or figurative mark 1 000 SEK
Application for registration of licence 1 000 SEK
Application of division of a trademark registration or application, for each divisional registration 1 800 SEK
Application for registration of pledge agreement or registration of new pledgee 1800 SEK
Reinstatement of dismissed application (according to chapter 2, article 20, second paragraph or chapter 2, article 36, third paragraph, VmL) 1 000 SEK
Application for revocation of trademark registration 900 SEK
An application for a change of company name (unchanged legal entity), an application for a change of attorney and an application for a change of address are free of charge.  

Other fees

Application for permission to use Swedish official designation 1 890 SEK
Registration certificates (NOTE - you will receive a registration certificate free of charge when your application is registered.) 200 SEK
Historical certificate of registration 350 SEK
Priority document 350 SEK
Registration fee 2 400 SEK

Fees for international trademark protection

Conversion of application for registration of a EU trademark or a collective mark, for protection in one class 3 000 SEK
For each additional class 1 000 SEK
Certificate relating to representation before EUIPO 1 200 SEK
Application for international registration for protection in a class of trademark or collective mark 3 900 SEK
For each additional class 1 000 SEK
Reinstatement of dismissed application (according to chapter 5, article 6, second paragraph VmL) 1 000 SEK

First, a basic fee is paid directly to the international agency. Then, applicants must pay either 1) a supplementary fee for each additional class after the first three and a complementary fee per designated country, or 2) an individual fee to the countries that have chosen to charge such a fee.

You can read more about the fees that apply on WIPO's website.
