Patent applications can be filed electronically via our E-filing System 24 hours a day from Monday to Sunday. Read more

Patent applications can also be sent to “Patents Registry, Intellectual Property Department, 24/F, Wu Chung House, 213 Queen's Road East, Wanchai, Hong Kong” by post or in person during the business hours 9:00 a.m. - 5:45 p.m. (Monday to Friday). Read more

A request to record or a request for registration and grant in respect of a standard patent (R) application can be sent to us by fax (fax no.: (852) 2838 6315) 24 hours a day from Monday to Friday.

Do not attempt to modify the forms, as doing so will result in an invalid form which cannot be accepted by the Registry.

Please pay the prescribed fee, if applicable, on submission of the form. We do NOT accept cash delivery (i.e. cash sent through mail). Read more

Important Reminder: Applicants and their employees or agents must not offer an advantage as defined in the Prevention of Bribery Ordinance (Cap. 201) to any government officer in connection with their applications or while having dealings of any kind with government departments.
