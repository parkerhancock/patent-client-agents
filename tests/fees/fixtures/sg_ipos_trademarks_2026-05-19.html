Forms and Fees

Forms, fees, and guidelines for managing trademarks in Singapore, covering applications, renewals, and other trademark-related services.

Last updated 3 January 2026

On this page

  • Trade mark forms
  • Forms related to filing under the Madrid Protocol
  • Forms for opposition, revocation or invalidation
  • Forms and procedures for other requests

Please proceed to our e-services portal, IPOS Digital Hub, to file your forms online. The acceptable modes of payment can be found here.

The Intellectual Property Office of Singapore has issued circulars on upcoming fee updates. In general, the majority of the fee updates will take effect on 1 September 2025, and the other fee updates will take effect on 1 April 2026. Please refer to our News page for more information.

Refund

Refund is generally not allowed. However, these exceptions may apply:

  • You have made duplicate payment for a form.

  • You have filed duplicate forms.

  • You have submitted a form which cannot be processed.

  • The IPOS examiner has not started work on your request for a search and/or examination report for a patent application.

Submit your request via IPOS Digital Hub.


Please note that forms which have been processed are generally not refundable.

Request for refund is subject to approval. Submission of a request does not guarantee a refund.

You are advised to check your forms carefully before submitting them.
