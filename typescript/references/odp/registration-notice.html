Register to access the USPTO's Open Data Portal

Published on: 06/03/2026 9:19 AM

USPTO Alert

Registration required soon for USPTO's Open Data Portal

The United States Patent and Trademark Office's (USPTO) Open Data Portal (ODP) offers the public a wide array of patent and trademark data and is one of the world's largest repositories of scientific, technical, and commercial information. In the last year, we've added new datasets to the ODP and updated many features.

Starting June 18, 2026, you'll need to have a USPTO.gov account to access, search, and download these datasets. This new effort will help improve security and performance, and lead to enhanced offerings in the future. 

​If you do not have a USPTO.gov account, you will need to register and create one for ODP access. If you already have a USPTO.gov account, no action is required—you'll continue to have full access to ODP's data without interruption after signing into your account. For existing accounts, you may be prompted to update fields in your profile. ​

Thank you for helping keep this data platform safe, fast, and reliable. ​To learn more, check out our information page or FAQs | Open Data Portal.  ​

​If you have any questions or feedback, please email us at data@uspto.gov. Your feedback can help shape future offerings.
