## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Mauritius has been a member of WIPO since 1976 and is a signatory to over 7 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Mauritius are the Ministry of Arts and Cultural Heritage and the Industrial Property Office of Mauritius (IPOM). The offices are located in Port Louis.
