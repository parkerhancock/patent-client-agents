## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Senegal has been a member of WIPO since 1970 and is a signatory to over 16 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Senegal are the Ministry of Culture, Société Sénégalaise du Droit d’Auteur et des Droits Voisins (SODAV) and Senegalese Agency for Industrial Property and Technological Innovation (ASPIT). The offices are located in Dakar.
