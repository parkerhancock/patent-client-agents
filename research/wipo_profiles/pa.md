## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Panama has been a member of WIPO since 1983 and is a signatory to over 13 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Panama are the National Directorate of Copyright and Directorate General of the Industrial Property Registry (DIGERPI). The offices are located in Panama City.
