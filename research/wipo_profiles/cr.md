## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Costa Rica has been a member of WIPO since 1981 and is a signatory to over 16 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Costa Rica is the Registro Nacional. The office is located in San José.
