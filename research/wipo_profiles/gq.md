## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Equatorial Guinea has been a member of WIPO since 1997 and is a signatory to over 4 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Equatorial Guinea is the Scientific and Technological Research Council (CICTE). The office is located in Malabo.
