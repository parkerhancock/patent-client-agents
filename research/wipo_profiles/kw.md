## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Kuwait has been a member of WIPO since 1998 and is a signatory to over 3 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Kuwait are the National Library of Kuwait and the Trademarks and Patents Department at the Ministry of Commerce and Industry. The offices are located in Kuwait City.
