## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Comoros has been a member of WIPO since 2005 and is a signatory to over 7 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Comoros are the Ministry of Youth, Employment, Workforce Development, Culture, and Sport and the Comorian Intellectual Property Office. The offices are located in Moroni.
