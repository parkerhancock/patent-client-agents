## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

The Republic of Moldova has been a member of WIPO since 1991 and is a signatory to over 26 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of the Republic of Moldova is the State Agency on Intellectual Property (AGEPI). The office is located in Chisinau.
