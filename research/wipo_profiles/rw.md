## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Rwanda has been a member of WIPO since 1984 and is a signatory to over 8 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Rwanda is the Office of the Registrar General, Rwanda Development Board (RDB). The office is located in Kigali.
