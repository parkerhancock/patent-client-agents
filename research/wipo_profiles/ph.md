## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

The Philippines has been a member of WIPO since 1980 and is a signatory to over 12 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of the Philippines is the Intellectual Property Office of the Philippines (IPOPHL). The office is located in Taguig.
