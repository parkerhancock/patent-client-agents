## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Zimbabwe has been a member of WIPO since 1981 and is a signatory to over 8 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Zimbabwe is the Companies and Intellectual Property Office of Zimbabwe (CIPZ). The office is located in Harare.
