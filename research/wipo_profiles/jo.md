## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Jordan has been a member of WIPO since 1972 and is a signatory to over 11 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Jordan are the Department of the National Library at the Ministry of Culture and the Industrial Property Protection Directorate. The offices are located in Amman.
