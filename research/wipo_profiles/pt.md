## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Portugal has been a member of WIPO since 1975 and is a signatory to over 22 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Portugal are the Bureau for Cultural Strategy, Planning and Assessment (GEPAC) and the Portuguese Institute of Industrial Property. The offices are located in Lisbon.
