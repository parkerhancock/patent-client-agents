## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Guinea-Bissau has been a member of WIPO since 1988 and is a signatory to over 3 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Guinea-Bissau are the Guinean Copyright Society and the General Directorate of Industrial Property. The offices are located in Bissau.
