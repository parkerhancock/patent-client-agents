## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Ecuador has been a member of WIPO since 1988 and is a signatory to over 11 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Ecuador is the National Service of Intellectual Rights (SENADI). The office is located in Quito.
