## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Gabon has been a member of WIPO since 1975 and is a signatory to over 10 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Gabon are the Copyright and Neighboring Rights Office of Gabon (BUGADA) and Industrial Property Office. The offices are located in Libreville.
