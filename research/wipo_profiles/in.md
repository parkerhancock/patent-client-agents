## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

India has been a member of WIPO since 1975 and is a signatory to over 16 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of India are the Copyright Office and the Office of the Controller General of Patents, Designs and Trademarks. The offices are located in New Delhi and in Mumbai respectively.
