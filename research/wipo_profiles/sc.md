## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Seychelles has been a member of WIPO since 2000 and is a signatory to over 3 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Seychelles is the Intellectual Property Office at the Department of Legal Affairs. The office is located in Victoria.
