## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

South Africa has been a member of WIPO since 1975 and is a signatory to over 9 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of South Africa is the Companies and Intellectual Property Commission (CIPC). The office is located in Pretoria.
