## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Austria has been a member of WIPO since 1973 and is a signatory to over 22 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Austria are the Federal Ministry of Justice and Austrian Patent Office. The offices are located in Vienna.
