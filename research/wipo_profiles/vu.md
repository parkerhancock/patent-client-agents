## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Vanuatu has been a member of WIPO since 2012 and is a signatory to over 6 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Vanuatu is Vanuatu Intellectual Property Office. The office is located in Port Vila.
