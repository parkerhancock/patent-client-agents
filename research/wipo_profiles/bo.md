## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Bolivia has been a member of WIPO since 1993 and is a signatory to over 9 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Bolivia is the National Intellectual Property Service (SENAPI). The office is located in La Paz.
