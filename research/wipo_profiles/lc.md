## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Saint Lucia has been a member of WIPO since 1993 and is a signatory to over 11 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Saint Lucia is the Registry of Companies and Intellectual Property. The office is located in Castries.
