## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

The United Kingdom has been a member of WIPO since 1970 and is a signatory to over 22 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of the United Kingdom is the Intellectual Property Office. The office is located in Newport, South Wales.
