## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Namibia has been a member of WIPO since 1991 and is a signatory to over 11 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Namibia is the Business and Intellectual Property Authority (BIPA). The office is located in Windhoek.
