## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

The Federated States of Micronesia has been a member of WIPO since 2025 and is a signatory to 2 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of the Federated States of Micronesia is the Department of Foreign Affairs. The office is located in Palikir.
