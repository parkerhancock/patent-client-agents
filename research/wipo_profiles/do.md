## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

The Dominican Republic has been a member of WIPO since 2000 and is a signatory to over 15 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of the Dominican Republic are the National Copyright Office (ONDA) and National Office of Industrial Property (ONAPI). The office is located in Santo Domingo.
