## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Belgium has been a member of WIPO since 1975 and is a signatory to over 21 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Belgium is the Belgian Intellectual Property Office (IPObel). The office is located in Brussels.
