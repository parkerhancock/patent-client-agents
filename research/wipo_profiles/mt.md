## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Malta has been a member of WIPO since 1977 and is a signatory to over 6 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Malta is the Industrial Property Registrations Directorate. The office is located in Valletta.
