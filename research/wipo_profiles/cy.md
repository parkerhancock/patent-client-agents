## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Cyprus has been a member of WIPO since 1984 and is a signatory to over 14 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Cyprus is the Department of Registrar of Companies and Intellectual Property at the Ministry of Energy, Commerce and Industry. The office is located in Nicosia.
