## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Nauru has been a member of WIPO since 2020 and is a signatory to over 2 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Nauru is the Registrar of Copyright, Trademarks and Patents at the Department of Justice and Border Control. The office is located in Aiwo.
