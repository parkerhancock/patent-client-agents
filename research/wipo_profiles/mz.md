## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Mozambique has been a member of WIPO since 1996 and is a signatory to over 8 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Mozambique are the National Institute of Culture and Creative Industries and the Industrial Property Institute (IPI). The offices are located in Maputo.
