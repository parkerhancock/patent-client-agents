## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Bulgaria has been a member of WIPO since 1970 and is a signatory to over 21 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Bulgaria are the Copyright and Neighbouring Rights Directorate and the Patent Office of Republic of Bulgaria. The offices are located in Sofia.
