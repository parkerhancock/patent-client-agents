## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Italy has been a member of WIPO since 1977 and is a signatory to over 24 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Italy are the Directorate General for Libraries and Copyright and the Markets and Consumers Protection Department of the Italian Patent and Trademark Office. The offices are located in Rome.
