## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Fiji has been a member of WIPO since 1972 and is a signatory to over 4 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Fiji is the Office of the Attorney-General. The office is located in Suva.
