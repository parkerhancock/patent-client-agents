## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Georgia has been a member of WIPO since 1991 and is a signatory to over 13 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Georgia is the National Intellectual Property Center (SAKPATENTI). The office is located in Mtskheta.
