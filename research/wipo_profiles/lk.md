## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Sri Lanka has been a member of WIPO since 1978 and is a signatory to over 7 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Sri Lanka is the National Intellectual Property Office of Sri Lanka. The office is located in Colombo.
