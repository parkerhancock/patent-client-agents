## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Dominica has been a member of WIPO since 1998 and is a signatory to over 5 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Dominica is the Companies and Intellectual Property Office (CIPO). The office is located in Roseau.
