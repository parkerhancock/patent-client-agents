## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Guatemala has been a member of WIPO since 1983 and is a signatory to over 15 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Guatemala is the Registry of Intellectual Property of Guatemala. The office is located in Guatemala City.
