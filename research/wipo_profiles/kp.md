## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

The Democratic People's Republic of Korea has been a member of WIPO since 1974 and is a signatory to over 19 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of the Democratic People's Republic of Korea is the Intellectual Property Administration (IPA). The office is located in Pyongyang.
