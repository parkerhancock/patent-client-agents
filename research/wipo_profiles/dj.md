## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Djibouti has been a member of WIPO since 2002 and is a signatory to over 6 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Djibouti are the Djibouti Office of Copyright and Related Rights and the Djiboutian Office of Industrial and Commercial Property (ODPIC). The offices are located in Djibouti.
