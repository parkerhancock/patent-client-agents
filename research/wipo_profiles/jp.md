## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Japan has been a member of WIPO since 1975 and is a signatory to over 20 WIPO treaties.

WIPO has an external office located in Tokyo.

The national IP offices of Japan are Japan Copyright Office (JCO) and Japan Patent Office (JPO). The offices are located in Tokyo.
