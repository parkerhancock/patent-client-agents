## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Algeria has been a member of WIPO since 1975 and is a signatory to over 16 WIPO treaties.

WIPO has an external office located in Algiers.

The national IP offices of Algeria are the National Office of Copyrights and Related Rights (ONDA) and Algerian National Institute of Industrial Property (INAPI). The offices are located in Algiers.
