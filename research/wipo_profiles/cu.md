## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Cuba has been a member of WIPO since 1975 and is a signatory to over 15 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Cuba are the National Copyright Center (CENDA) and the Cuban Industrial Property Office (OCPI). The offices are located in La Habana.
