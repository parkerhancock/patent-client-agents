## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Myanmar has been a member of WIPO since 2001 and is a signatory to 1 WIPO treaty.

The national IP office of Myanmar is the Intellectual Property Department (IPD) of the Ministry of Commerce. The office is located in Nay Pyi Taw.
