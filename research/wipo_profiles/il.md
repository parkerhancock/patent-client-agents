## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Israel has been a member of WIPO since 1970 and is a signatory to over 20 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Israel are the Office of the Director General at the Ministry of Justice and the Israel Patent Office. The offices are located in Jerusalem.
