## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Saint Vincent and the Grenadines has been a member of WIPO since 1995 and is a signatory to over 9 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Saint Vincent and the Grenadines is the Commerce and Intellectual Property Office. The office is located in Kingstown.
