## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Kenya has been a member of WIPO since 1971 and is a signatory to over 17 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Kenya are the Kenya Copyright Board and the Kenya Industrial Property Institute (KIPI). The offices are located in Nairobi.
