## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Cook Islands has been a member of WIPO since 2016 and is a signatory to over 5 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Cook Islands is the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Immigration. The office is located in Avarua.
