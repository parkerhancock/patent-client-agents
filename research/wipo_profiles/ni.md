## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Nicaragua has been a member of WIPO since 1985 and is a signatory to over 15 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Nicaragua is the Intellectual Property Registry (RPI). The office is located in Managua.
