## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Libya has been a member of WIPO since 1976 and is a signatory to over 3 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Libya is the Libyan Authority for Research at the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research. The office is located in Tripoli.
