## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Lebanon has been a member of WIPO since 1986 and is a signatory to over 10 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Lebanon is the Office of Intellectual Property. The office is located in Beirut.
