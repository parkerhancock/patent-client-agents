## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Togo has been a member of WIPO since 1975 and is a signatory to over 16 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Togo are the Togolese Office of Copyright and Neighboring Rights (BUTODRA) and the National Institute for Industrial Property and Technology (INPIT). The offices are located in Lomé.
