## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Marshall Islands has been a member of WIPO since 2017 and is a signatory to over 3 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Marshall Islands is the Ministry of Culture and Internal Affairs. The office is located in Majuro.
