## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Cabo Verde has been a member of WIPO since 1997 and is a signatory to over 11 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Cabo Verde is the Institute for Quality Management and Intellectual Property (IGQPI). The office is located in Praia.
