## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Botswana has been a member of WIPO since 1998 and is a signatory to over 9 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Botswana is the Companies and Intellectual Property Authority (CIPA). The office is located in Gaborone.
