## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Finland has been a member of WIPO since 1970 and is a signatory to over 19 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Finland are the Division for Copyright Policy and Audiovisual Culture at the Ministry of Education and Culture and the Finnish Patent and Registration Office (PRH). The offices are located in Helsinki.
