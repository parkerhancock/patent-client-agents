## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Côte d'Ivoire has been a member of WIPO since 1974 and is a signatory to over 13 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Côte d'Ivoire are the Ivorian Copyright Office and the Ivorian Office of Intellectual Property. The offices are located in Abidjan.
