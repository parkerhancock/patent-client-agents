## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Serbia has been a member of WIPO since 1992 and is a signatory to over 25 treaties.

The national IP office of Serbia is the Intellectual Property Office of the Republic of Serbia. The office is located in Belgrade.
