## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Colombia has been a member of WIPO since 1980 and is a signatory to over 16 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Colombia are the National Directorate of Copyright and the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce. The offices are located in Bogotá.
