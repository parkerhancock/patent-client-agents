## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Solomon Islands has been a member of WIPO since 2019 and is a signatory to 2 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Solomon Islands is the Registrar-General's Office at the Ministry of Justice and Legal Affairs. The office is located in Honiara.
