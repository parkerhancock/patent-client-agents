## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Australia has been a member of WIPO since 1972 and is a signatory to over 18 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Australia are the Attorney-General’s Department and IP Australia. The offices are located in Canberra.
