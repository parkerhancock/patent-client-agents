## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Spain has been a member of WIPO since 1970 and is a signatory to over 23 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Spain are the Ministry of Culture and the Spanish Patent and Trademark Office (OEPM). The offices are located in Madrid.
