## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Barbados has been a member of WIPO since 1979 and is a signatory to over 10 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Barbados is the Corporate Affairs and Intellectual Property Office (CAIPO). The office is located in Warrens.
