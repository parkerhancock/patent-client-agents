## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Mali has been a member of WIPO since 1982 and is a signatory to over 9 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Mali are the Malian Copyright Office and the Malian Centre for the Promotion of Industrial Property (CEMAPI). The offices are located in Bamako.
