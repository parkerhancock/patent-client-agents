## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Suriname has been a member of WIPO since 1975 and is a signatory to over 5 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Suriname is the Intellectual Property Office of Suriname. The office is located in Paramaribo.
