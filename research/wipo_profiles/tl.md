## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Timor-Leste has been a member of WIPO since 2017 and is a signatory to 1 WIPO treaty.

The national IP office of Timor-Leste is the Permanent Mission of the Democratic Republic of Timor-Leste to the United Nations Office at Geneva. The office is located in Geneva, Switzerland.
