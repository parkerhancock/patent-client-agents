## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Qatar has been a member of WIPO since 1976 and is a signatory to over 13 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Qatar is the Intellectual Property Department at the Ministry of Commerce and Industry. The office is located in Doha.
