## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Chad has been a member of WIPO since 1970 and is a signatory to over 5 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Chad are the Chadian Copyright Office (BUTDRA) and the Ministry of Commerce and Industry (SNL-OAPI-TCHAD). The offices are located in N'Djaména.
