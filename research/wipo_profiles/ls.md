## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Lesotho has been a member of WIPO since 1986 and is a signatory to over 8 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Lesotho is the Registrar General's Office (RGO). The office is located in Maseru.
