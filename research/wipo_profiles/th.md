## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Thailand has been a member of WIPO since 1989 and is a signatory to over 6 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Thailand is the Department of Intellectual Property (DIP) at the Ministry of Commerce. The office is located in Nonthaburi.
