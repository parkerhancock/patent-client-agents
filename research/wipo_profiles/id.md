## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Indonesia has been a member of WIPO since 1979 and is a signatory to over 14 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Indonesia is the Directorate General of Intellectual Property (DGIP). The office is located in Jakarta.
