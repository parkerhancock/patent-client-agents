## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Luxembourg has been a member of WIPO since 1975 and is a signatory to over 20 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Luxembourg is the Intellectual Property Office. The office is located in Luxembourg.
