## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Haiti has been a member of WIPO since 1983 and is a signatory to over 7 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Haiti are the Haitian Copyright Office (BHDA) and the Ministry of Trade and Industry. The office is located in Port-au-Prince.
