## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

The Czech Republic has been a member of WIPO since 1993 and is a signatory to over 22 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of the Czech Republic are the Copyright Law Department at the Ministry of Culture and the Industrial Property Office of the Czech Republic. The offices are located in Prague.
