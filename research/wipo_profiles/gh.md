## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Ghana has been a member of WIPO since 1976 and is a signatory to over 18 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Ghana are the Copyright Department and the Registrar General's Department at the Ministry of Justice. The offices are located in Accra.
