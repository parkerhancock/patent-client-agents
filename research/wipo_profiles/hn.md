## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Honduras has been a member of WIPO since 1983 and is a signatory to over 12?nbsp;WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Honduras is the Institute of Intellectual Property (DIGEPIH). The office is located in Tegucigalpa.
