## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Bahamas has been a member of WIPO since 1977 and is a signatory to over 7 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Bahamas is the Registrar General's Department. The office is located in Nassau.
