## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Lithuania has been a member of WIPO since 1992 and is a signatory to over 17 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Lithuania are the Media and Copyright Policy Unit at the Ministry of Culture and the State Patent Bureau. The offices are located in Vilnius.
