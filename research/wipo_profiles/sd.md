## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Sudan has been a member of WIPO since 1974 and is a signatory to over 9 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Sudan are the Council for Protection of Copyright and Related Rights and the Registrar General of Intellectual Property Administration. The offices are located in Omdurman and Khartoum respectively.
