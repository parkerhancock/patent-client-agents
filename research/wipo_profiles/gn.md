## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Guinea has been a member of WIPO since 1980 and is a signatory to over 13 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Guinea are the Guinean Copyright Office (BGDA) and the National Service of Industrial Property. The offices are located in Conakry.
