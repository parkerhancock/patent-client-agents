## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Bhutan has been a member of WIPO since 1994 and is a signatory to over 4 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Bhutan is the Department of Media Creative Industry and Industrial Property (DoMCIIP) at the Ministry of Industry Commerce and Employment. The office is located in Thimphu.
