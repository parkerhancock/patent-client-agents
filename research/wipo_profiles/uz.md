## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Uzbekistan has been a member of WIPO since 1991 and is a signatory to over 19 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Uzbekistan is the Ministry of Justice of the Republic of Uzbekistan. The office is located in Tashkent.
