## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Malaysia has been a member of WIPO since 1989 and is a signatory to over 10 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Malaysia is the Intellectual Property Corporation of Malaysia (MyIPO). The office is located in Petaling Jaya.
