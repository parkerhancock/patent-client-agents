## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

The Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela has been a member of WIPO since 1984 and is a signatory to over 7 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of the Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela is the Autonomous Service of Intellectual Property (SAPI). The office is located in Caracas.
