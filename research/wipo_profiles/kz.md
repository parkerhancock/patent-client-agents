## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Kazakhstan has been a member of WIPO since 1991 and is a signatory to over 18 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Kazakhstan are the Committee for Intellectual Property Rights and the National Institute of Intellectual Property. The offices are located in Astana.
