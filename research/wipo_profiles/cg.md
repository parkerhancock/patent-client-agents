## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Congo has been a member of WIPO since 1975 and is a signatory to over 11 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of Congo are the Congolese Copyright Office (BCDA) and the Directorate of the National Industrial Property Office. The offices are located in Brazzaville.
