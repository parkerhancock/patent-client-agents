## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

North Macedonia has been a member of WIPO since 1991 and is a signatory to over 21 WIPO treaties.

The national IP offices of North Macedonia are the Office for Normative and Administrative Affairs, Copyright and Related Rights at the Ministry of Culture and the State Office of Industrial Property (SOIP). The offices are located in Skopje.
