## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Sierra Leone has been a member of WIPO since 1986 and is a signatory to over 6 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Sierra Leone is the National Registry for Industrial Property and Copyright. The office is located in Freetown.
