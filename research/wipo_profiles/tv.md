## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Tuvalu has been a member of WIPO since 2014 and is a signatory to 2 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Tuvalu is the Office of the Attorney-General at the Ministry of Justice, Communications and Foreign Affairs. The office is located in Funafuti.
