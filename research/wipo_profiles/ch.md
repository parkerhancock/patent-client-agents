## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Switzerland has been a member of WIPO since 1970 and is a signatory to over 26 WIPO treaties.

WIPO headquarters are located in Geneva.

The national IP office of Switzerland is the Swiss Federal Institute of Intellectual Property (IPI). The office is located in Bern.
