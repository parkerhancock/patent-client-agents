## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Maldives has been a member of WIPO since 2004 and is a signatory to 2 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Maldives is the Intellectual Property Unit at the Ministry of Economic Development. The office is located in Male.
