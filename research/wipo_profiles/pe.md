## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Peru has been a member of WIPO since 1980 and is a signatory to over 21 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Peru is the National Institute for the Defense of Competition and Protection of Intellectual Property (INDECOPI). The office is located in Lima.
