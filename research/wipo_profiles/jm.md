## Lead summary (verbatim from WIPO page)

Explore IP Landscape:

Jamaica has been a member of WIPO since 1978 and is a signatory to over 15 WIPO treaties.

The national IP office of Jamaica is the Jamaica Intellectual Property Office (JIPO). The office is located in Kingston.
