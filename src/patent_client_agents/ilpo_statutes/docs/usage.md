# ILPO Israel statutes — usage

Five Israeli IP statutes are indexed in this corpus:

| Key | Citation form | Notes |
|-----|---------------|-------|
| `patents` | Patents Law, 5727-1967 | National patent statute. |
| `trademarks` | Trade Marks Ordinance [New Version], 5732-1972 | National TM statute. |
| `designs` | Designs Law, 5777-2017 | Replaced the old Patents and Designs Ordinance. |
| `copyright` | Copyright Act, 5768-2007 | Replaced the UK-era 1911 Copyright Act. |
| `commercial_torts` | Commercial Torts Law, 5759-1999 | **Trade-secret regime (Arts. 6-9) with statutory damages (Art. 13).** |

The distinctive piece is the **Commercial Torts Law** — Israel ships a
standalone statute for trade-secret protection, unregistered-mark
protection, and preliminary remedies, rather than tucking those rules
into the civil or commercial code. Articles 6-9 carry the trade-secret
core; Article 13 carries the up-to-NIS 100,000 statutory-damages
ceiling without proof of actual loss.

Citation forms accepted by `get_ilpo_section`:

- `Section 3 Patents Law`
- `Section 1 Trade Marks Ordinance`
- `Article 6 Commercial Torts Law`
- `Patents Law §3` (reverse order also works)

Source: WIPO Lex authoritative English translations prepared by ILPO
/ Israeli Ministry of Justice.
